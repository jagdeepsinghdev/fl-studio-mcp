# Scales and modes — the full catalogue

Step patterns: W = whole step (2 semitones), H = half step (1), A2 = augmented 2nd (3).
Semitone formulas from the root (0 = root). For ready-to-type **MIDI numbers in C** and key
transposition, use `fl-melody/references/scales.md` (the FL quick table); this page is the
complete theory catalogue.

## 1. Major and the diatonic modes

The 7 modes are rotations of the major scale. Learn each as **"a major or minor scale with
one colour note"**; that's how professionals hear them.

| Mode | Degree of major | Formula | Steps | vs major/minor | Colour note | Sound / use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ionian (major) | 1 | 0 2 4 5 7 9 11 | W W H W W W H | — | M7 + M3 | bright, resolved; pop, classical |
| Dorian | 2 | 0 2 3 5 7 9 10 | W H W W W H W | minor with ♮6 | **M6** | cool, soulful; funk, lo-fi, jazz-minor vamps, "So What" |
| Phrygian | 3 | 0 1 3 5 7 8 10 | H W W W H W W | minor with b2 | **b2** | dark, Spanish/Middle-Eastern; metal, drill, dark trap |
| Lydian | 4 | 0 2 4 6 7 9 11 | W W W H W W H | major with #4 | **#4** | dreamy, floating; film (Williams/Elfman), future bass |
| Mixolydian | 5 | 0 2 4 5 7 9 10 | W W H W W H W | major with b7 | **b7** | bluesy, rock, dominant 7 sound; reggaeton/Afro hooks |
| Aeolian (natural minor) | 6 | 0 2 3 5 7 8 10 | W H W W H W W | — | b6 + b3 | sad, dark; trap, pop ballads |
| Locrian | 7 | 0 1 3 5 6 8 10 | H W W H W W W | minor with b2 + b5 | **b5** | unstable (dim tonic); rare, metal, over m7b5 |

Brightness order (each step lowers one note): **Lydian > Ionian > Mixolydian > Dorian >
Aeolian > Phrygian > Locrian.** Useful when a part should be "a bit darker" or "a bit brighter".

**Relative vs parallel modes:** C Dorian uses the notes of Bb major (relative); comparing
C Dorian with C Aeolian (parallel) shows the colour note. Composers think parallel.

## 2. The minor family (classical)

| Scale | Formula | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| Natural minor | 0 2 3 5 7 8 10 | the pure modal minor |
| Harmonic minor | 0 2 3 5 7 8 **11** | raised 7th = leading tone → major **V** and **vii°7**; creates the A2 (8→11) "exotic" leap |
| Melodic minor (ascending / jazz minor) | 0 2 3 5 7 **9 11** | raised 6 and 7 smooth the melody upward; classical descends as natural minor; jazz uses it both ways |

### Modes of harmonic minor (most used)
| Mode | Formula | Name / use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 2 3 5 7 8 11 | harmonic minor: drill, horror, classical |
| 5 | 0 1 4 5 7 8 10 | **Phrygian dominant** (Spanish/Freygish/Hijaz-like): flamenco, Middle-Eastern, metal, drill melodies over V |
| 4 | 0 2 3 6 7 9 10 | Dorian #4 (Ukrainian Dorian, Romanian minor) |
| 6 | 0 3 4 6 7 9 11 | Lydian #2 |

### Modes of melodic minor (jazz chord-scale workhorses)
| Mode | Formula | Name | Plays over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 2 3 5 7 9 11 | melodic minor | m(maj7), m6 |
| 2 | 0 1 3 5 7 9 10 | Dorian b2 (Phrygian ♮6) | sus(b9) |
| 3 | 0 2 4 6 8 9 11 | Lydian augmented | maj7#5 |
| 4 | 0 2 4 6 7 9 10 | **Lydian dominant** (overtone/acoustic) | 7#11: the Simpsons theme, Bartók |
| 5 | 0 2 4 5 7 8 10 | Mixolydian b6 (Aeolian dominant) | 7b13 |
| 6 | 0 2 3 5 6 8 10 | Locrian ♮2 (half-diminished) | m7b5 |
| 7 | 0 1 3 4 6 8 10 | **Altered** (super-Locrian) | 7alt: b9 #9 #11 b13 over a resolving V |

## 3. Pentatonic and blues

| Scale | Formula | Notes in C | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major pentatonic | 0 2 4 7 9 | C D E G A | folk, country, pop hooks, Chinese/Japanese folk; no half steps = no clashes |
| Minor pentatonic | 0 3 5 7 10 | C Eb F G Bb | rock, blues, rap, R&B riffs; the safest scale over minor |
| Blues (minor) | 0 3 5 **6** 7 10 | C Eb F Gb G Bb | adds the b5 "blue note" passing tone |
| Major blues | 0 2 **3** 4 7 9 | C D Eb E G A | the b3→3 slide: gospel, soul, country |
| Egyptian (suspended) | 0 2 5 7 10 | C D F G Bb | open, sus sound |
| Japanese In (Miyako-bushi) | 0 1 5 7 8 | C Db F G Ab | koto-like, dark; anime/lo-fi |
| Hirajoshi | 0 2 3 7 8 | C D Eb G Ab | Japanese, eerie |
| Yo | 0 2 5 7 9 | C D F G A | Japanese folk, bright |

Pro tip: over a **minor** chord, minor pentatonic from the root; over a **dominant 7**, major
pentatonic from the root + the blues b3 as a grace note; over a **major 7**, major pentatonic
from the **5th** (C maj7 → G major pentatonic gives the 9 and #11 colours).

## 4. Symmetric scales

| Scale | Formula | Property | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chromatic | all 12 | — | passing runs, glissandi |
| Whole-tone | 0 2 4 6 8 10 | only 2 transpositions; no P5 | dreamy/blurry (Debussy), 7#5 chords, "dream sequence" |
| Diminished (half-whole) | 0 1 3 4 6 7 9 10 | 3 transpositions; repeats every m3 | over 7b9 dominants: jazz, film tension |
| Diminished (whole-half) | 0 2 3 5 6 8 9 11 | | over dim7 chords |
| Augmented (hexatonic) | 0 3 4 7 8 11 | repeats every M3 | Coltrane, modern film |

Messiaen catalogued 7 "modes of limited transposition"; whole-tone is #1, octatonic (diminished) is #2.

## 5. Bebop scales (jazz; add a chromatic passing tone so chord tones land on beats)
- Bebop dominant: Mixolydian + M7 (0 2 4 5 7 9 10 11).
- Bebop major: major + #5/b6 (0 2 4 5 7 8 9 11).
- Bebop minor (Dorian): Dorian + M3 (0 2 3 4 5 7 9 10).

Ragas are more than scales: they have characteristic phrases (pakad), ascending/descending
rules (aroh/avroh), emphasised notes (vadi/samvadi) and time-of-day associations.

## 7. Choosing a scale (professional method)

1. **Chord-scale:** each chord implies a scale (jazz pedagogy, Berklee). Over Cm7 in a ii-V in
   Bb → C Dorian; over Cm as i in C minor → C Aeolian. The context decides, not the chord alone.
2. **Avoid notes:** a scale note a half step above a chord tone, held on a strong beat, clashes
   (the 4th over a major chord, the b9 over a minor chord). Use them as passing tones only.
3. **Colour note first:** to establish a mode, put its colour note on a strong beat or over a
   pedal on the tonic. Without it, modes collapse into plain major/minor.
4. **Genre defaults:** trap/drill = Aeolian, Phrygian, harmonic minor; lo-fi/neo-soul = Dorian
   + extended chords; pop = Ionian/Aeolian; rock = Mixolydian/minor pentatonic; reggaeton =
   Aeolian/Phrygian; house = Aeolian/Dorian; film = Lydian (wonder), Phrygian (threat), whole-tone (dream).
